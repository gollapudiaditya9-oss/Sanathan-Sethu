# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hindu families and ritual-service partners across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Families use SanatanaSetu to find verified Purohits, consultants, and related ceremony services. Partners use it to reach suitable families without managing booking coordination alone. Telugu is primary, English is secondary, and ritual terminology may appear in Sanskrit.

## Product Purpose

SanatanaSetu is a pre-launch marketplace that connects families with verified Purohits, consultants, caterers, samagri suppliers, venues, and transport providers. The site must establish trust, explain how the service works, and let families or partners take the next clear step without inflated claims.

## Brand Personality

Plain, composed, trustworthy. The voice is direct and factual. Promises are specific, sentences end cleanly, and the experience feels culturally grounded without becoming ornamental.

## Anti-references

No generic marketplace styling, invented social proof, conversion tricks, chat widgets, sticky calls to action, inflated marketing language, decorative spiritual imagery, or fabricated people and numbers. The experience must not resemble a templated software landing page.

## Design Principles

1. Earn trust through specific, testable information.
2. Make culturally important terms clear without flattening them.
3. Keep every action direct and every promise operationally credible.
4. Preserve the locked V3 visual language and its deliberate restraint.
5. Prefer correctness and calm over novelty or speed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA and maintain strong keyboard, focus, and screen-reader behavior. Respect reduced-motion preferences, never rely on color alone to communicate state, and preserve readable hierarchy across desktop and mobile layouts.
